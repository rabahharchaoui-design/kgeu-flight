Controls help

Left thumb anywhere on the left half: a stick appears there. Push to roll and climb or dive, let go and it springs back. Right edge: the throttle, slide it or tap IDLE, CLIMB, FULL. BRAKE above it, hold on the ground. Top right: Auto takes off or lands for you, then gear, flaps and camera. Pause is top centre. Tap the grade badge after a landing to see the details.
